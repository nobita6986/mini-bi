or checksums. The Direct Entry session response is intentionally an actor
projection; **do not send or paste raw JSON, identifiers, cookies, headers,

PASS/FAIL and, on failure, the sanitized error code/status. Do not perform
POST/PATCH/PUT/DELETE requests. Verify “Nhập liệu” remains absent from the
