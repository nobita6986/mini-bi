# P3.1 — Full User and Catalog Administration

> **Status:** `C01_POLICY_LOCKED_READY_FOR_W01`
> **Authority:** Owner decisions recorded on 2026-10-06 and 2026-10-09; T0 C01 lock on 2026-10-09.
> **Prerequisite:** P2/P3 Release A and P2.5 Release B are deployed, verified and accepted.
> **Scope warning:** P3.1 manages business catalogs and existing application identities. Invite/account creation, forced reset, Admin reset, MFA and recovery remain P3.2. AI activation remains P3.3.

P3.1 replaces the temporary workbook/bootstrap operating model with audited browser administration for personnel, teams, team leaders, projects, vendors and existing application access. It reuses the current Direct Entry catalog, grants/scopes and immutable audit foundations; it must not introduce a second RBAC or generic CRUD framework.

## 1. Locked business outcomes

- Admin and Accounting can create, edit, activate/deactivate and administer the business catalogs for HRP personnel, teams, team-leader assignments, projects, vendors and labor types.
- A new HRP personnel record may exist without an application account and may be assigned to one team or left unassigned.
- Team assignment is effective-dated history. Moving or removing a member closes the previous interval; it never rewrites historical reporting attribution.
- A team leader is an enabled, login-backed application user linked to an active HRP recruiter. Free-text leaders are forbidden.
- Leader authority and the Team Dashboard `team` scope must come from the same server-authorized operation. `recruiters.personnel_position` is a display/catalog attribute, not authorization authority.
- A team leader may assign or unassign project managers only when the target manager is an effective member of the leader's own team. The project may be any active project because projects remain team-agnostic. This authority does not grant project create/update/activate/deactivate or cross-team manager administration.
- Project and Vendor records use activate/deactivate semantics. Referenced records are never hard-deleted.
- Labor type becomes an administered catalog rather than a browser-entered free-text field. Existing `TEMPORARY`/`PERMANENT` values remain stable and P3.1 seeds `OUTSOURCED` with the display label `Gia công`.
- Projects remain provider-agnostic: any HRP or Vendor may supply workers to any active project. Project-manager assignments are independent from team leaders.
- Vendor records have no business team or team-leader relationship.
- Every mutation requires an explicit reason, expected version, idempotency key and immutable actor/time/before/after audit evidence.

## 2. Authority boundaries

| Surface | Admin | Accounting | Team leader | Other actors |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Personnel/team/leader catalog | Full | Full | Read own team as operationally required | Read only when required by operational scope |
| Project/vendor catalog | Full | Full | Read active projects needed for assignment | Scoped operational read only |
| Labor-type catalog | Full | Full | Read active values | Read active values for operational input/filtering |
| Project-manager assignments | Full | Full | Assign/unassign effective members of own team only; any active project | Manager receives resulting project authority only |
| Existing account enable/disable | Full | No | No | Self-service password only |
| Capability/scope grants | Full | No | No direct grant management | No |
| App-user ↔ recruiter links | Full | No | No | No |
| Audit explorer | Full | Catalog/worker events allowed by policy | Own assignment mutations only | No unrestricted PII |
| `entry_restore` | Full | No | No | No |
| `pii_export` | Only after explicit Owner approval | No by default | No | No |

The current full-Admin predicate is the AND of `entry_admin`, `recruiter_master_manage` and `team_master_manage` at `all` scope. Accounting must not receive that complete bundle merely to operate catalogs. C01 locks the narrow `catalog_master_manage` capability at effective `all` scope for Admin and Accounting business-catalog mutations while preserving the existing Admin-only account/grant boundary.

Team-leader manager-assignment authority uses the separate `team_manager_assign` capability, provisioned and revoked atomically with the effective leader designation and `team` scope. It must not reuse `entry_admin`, `catalog_master_manage` or a role-name/client check. The server predicate is capability + effective `team` scope + verified recruiter link + effective membership in that same team.

## 3. Catalog lifecycle contracts

### Personnel, teams and leaders

- Personnel creation writes the existing `recruiters` identity plus HRP provider membership; account creation is not implied.
- `personnel_code`, display name, position and active state remain versioned business attributes.
- Zero active team memberships is valid in the Admin catalog. More than one active team membership remains invalid.
- An unassigned person is visible in administration but excluded fail-closed from team attribution and operational recruiter selection where the current Direct Entry contract requires exactly one team.
- Assign, move and unassign operations reuse `recruiter_team_memberships` with half-open `[valid_from, valid_to)` intervals.
- Designating a leader requires an enabled app user, one verified recruiter link and an effective membership in the target team. The operation creates/ends the corresponding `team` scope grant and narrow project-manager-assignment capability atomically and records a reason/audit event.
- A team has at most one active leader. Historical leader intervals are preserved; appointing a replacement closes the prior leader interval atomically before opening the new one. Co-leaders are outside the locked P3.1 contract.

### Projects, project managers and vendors

- P2.5 owns the go-live minimum for project create/update/deactivate and effective-dated multiple-manager assignment.
- P3.1 exposes the full audited browser administration surface and may extend project metadata only after fields are explicitly contracted; it must not add speculative columns.
- Admin and Accounting retain all-project manager-assignment authority through the accepted catalog-operator/admin contract.
- A team leader may list manager candidates only from the leader's own effective team and may assign those candidates to any active project. Self-assignment is allowed only when the leader is also an eligible effective member of that team.
- A team leader may unassign an open assignment only when its manager is an effective member of that leader's team at authorization time. This includes cancelling a future-dated open assignment; the canonical half-open interval collapses to `valid_to = valid_from`. Moving a manager to another team does not rewrite assignment history; subsequent administration follows the manager's current effective team.
- Assign requires an active project. Unassign remains allowed for an open assignment on an inactive project so authority can always be revoked; an already closed assignment replays as the existing no-op result.
- Leader assignment reads and mutations must be server-scoped. Candidate, assignment and audit projections must not leak cross-team identities. Direct URL/API/RPC calls for another team fail closed.
- The existing assignment interval, project/assignment OCC, reason, idempotency, revision and immutable audit semantics remain unchanged. Audit records the actual capability and `team` scope for leader actions rather than mislabeling them as `entry_admin@all`.
- Leader authority does not permit creating, renaming, activating or deactivating projects, editing project metadata, managing team membership, or granting capabilities/scopes directly.
- Vendor administration reuses `vendors`, the Vendor recruiter representation and `recruiter_provider_memberships.vendor_id`. `vendor_id` is chosen at creation and immutable; `display_name` and `active` are mutable with OCC/audit.
- Vendor creation atomically creates its canonical recruiter/provider representation without a team membership. Renaming, activating or deactivating a Vendor must keep historical Direct Entry facts resolvable; deactivation removes it from new-entry choices without deleting or rewriting historical membership/facts.
- Project and Vendor mutations are server/RPC authorized. Browser state is never the permission boundary.

### Labor types

- Reuse `direct_entries.labor_type` as the persisted stable catalog key; do not turn it into arbitrary free text and do not rewrite historical entries.
- Seed the canonical keys `TEMPORARY`, `PERMANENT` and `OUTSOURCED`, displayed as `Thời vụ`, `Chính thức` and `Gia công` with initial sort orders `10`, `20`, `30`.
- Admin/Accounting may add types and rename/reorder/activate/deactivate every display label, including the three seeded rows, through audited server/RPC mutations. A key is immutable after creation. A referenced type is never hard-deleted, and deactivation only removes it from new-entry choices.
- `labor_type` remains independent from `provider_type`, recruiter provider membership and team attribution. Selecting `Gia công` must not imply Vendor or a particular team/provider.
- Direct Entry, change requests, imports/templates and reporting dimensions must resolve the same catalog projection; unknown or inactive values fail closed for new writes while historical values remain readable.

### Accounts, grants and links

- P3.1 manages application accounts that already exist: enable/disable, grant/scope intervals and verified recruiter links.
- Disabling/offboarding revokes effective access without deleting the app user, recruiter identity, facts or audit history.
- Account invitation/creation and recovery credentials remain P3.2 even when the personnel catalog record is created in P3.1.
- No authorization decision may depend on role name, email suffix, client-supplied actor fields or environment fallbacks.

## 4. Reuse and minimum delta

| Accounts/grants | App users, capability grants, scope grants, actor repository | Admin-only server mutations and revocation-safe UI |
| Audit/reason/OCC | Restricted reasons, revisions, audit events, RPC idempotency | Catalog entity projections; no new audit framework |

No new ORM, RBAC framework, form/state framework, grid library, workflow engine or audit dependency is allowed without a source-backed gap and Owner approval.
