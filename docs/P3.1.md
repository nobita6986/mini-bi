# P3.1 — Full User and Catalog Administration

> **Status:** `PLANNED_AFTER_COMBINED_P2_P3_P2_5_CLOSURE`  
> **Authority:** Owner decisions recorded on 2026-10-06.  
> **Prerequisite:** P2/P3 Release A and P2.5 Release B are deployed, verified and accepted.  
> **Scope warning:** P3.1 manages business catalogs and existing application identities. Invite/account creation, forced reset, Admin reset, MFA and recovery remain P3.2. AI activation remains P3.3.

P3.1 replaces the temporary workbook/bootstrap operating model with audited browser administration for personnel, teams, team leaders, projects, vendors and existing application access. It reuses the current Direct Entry catalog, grants/scopes and immutable audit foundations; it must not introduce a second RBAC or generic CRUD framework.

## 1. Locked business outcomes

- Admin and Accounting can create, edit, activate/deactivate and administer the business catalogs for HRP personnel, teams, team-leader assignments, projects, vendors and labor types.
- A new HRP personnel record may exist without an application account and may be assigned to one team or left unassigned.
- Team assignment is effective-dated history. Moving or removing a member closes the previous interval; it never rewrites historical reporting attribution.
- A team leader is an enabled, login-backed application user linked to an active HRP recruiter. Free-text leaders are forbidden.
- Leader authority and the Team Dashboard `team` scope must come from the same server-authorized operation. `recruiters.personnel_position` is a display/catalog attribute, not authorization authority.
- Project and Vendor records use activate/deactivate semantics. Referenced records are never hard-deleted.
- Labor type becomes an administered catalog rather than a browser-entered free-text field. Existing `TEMPORARY`/`PERMANENT` values remain stable and P3.1 seeds `OUTSOURCED` with the display label `Gia công`.
- Projects remain provider-agnostic: any HRP or Vendor may supply workers to any active project. Project-manager assignments are independent from team leaders.
- Vendor records have no business team or team-leader relationship.
- Every mutation requires an explicit reason, expected version, idempotency key and immutable actor/time/before/after audit evidence.

## 2. Authority boundaries

| Surface | Admin | Accounting | Other actors |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personnel/team/leader catalog | Full | Full | Read only when required by operational scope |
| Project/vendor catalog | Full | Full | Scoped operational read only |
| Labor-type catalog | Full | Full | Read active values for operational input/filtering |
| Project-manager assignments | Full | Full | Manager receives resulting project authority only |
| Existing account enable/disable | Full | No | Self-service password only |
| Capability/scope grants | Full | No | No |
| App-user ↔ recruiter links | Full | No | No |
| Audit explorer | Full | Catalog/worker events allowed by policy | No unrestricted PII |
| `entry_restore` | Full | Only if the P3.1 policy explicitly retains it | No |
| `pii_export` | Only after explicit Owner approval | No by default | No |

The current full-Admin predicate is the AND of `entry_admin`, `recruiter_master_manage` and `team_master_manage` at `all` scope. Accounting must not receive that complete bundle merely to operate catalogs. P3.1-C01 must lock a narrow catalog-operator capability contract—preferably one explicit `catalog_master_manage@all` contract bump reused across personnel/team/project/vendor RPCs—while preserving the existing Admin-only account/grant boundary. The exact token name is not authoritative until C01 is accepted.

## 3. Catalog lifecycle contracts

### Personnel, teams and leaders

- Personnel creation writes the existing `recruiters` identity plus HRP provider membership; account creation is not implied.
- `personnel_code`, display name, position and active state remain versioned business attributes.
- Zero active team memberships is valid in the Admin catalog. More than one active team membership remains invalid.
- An unassigned person is visible in administration but excluded fail-closed from team attribution and operational recruiter selection where the current Direct Entry contract requires exactly one team.
- Assign, move and unassign operations reuse `recruiter_team_memberships` with half-open `[valid_from, valid_to)` intervals.
- Designating a leader requires an enabled app user, one verified recruiter link and an effective membership in the target team. The operation creates/ends the corresponding `team` scope grant atomically and records a reason/audit event.
- C01 must decide whether a team supports one active primary leader or multiple active leaders. Do not infer this cardinality from the current seven-row bootstrap.

### Projects and vendors

- P2.5 owns the go-live minimum for project create/update/deactivate and effective-dated multiple-manager assignment.
- P3.1 exposes the full audited browser administration surface and may extend project metadata only after fields are explicitly contracted; it must not add speculative columns.
- Vendor administration reuses `vendors`, the Vendor recruiter representation and `recruiter_provider_memberships.vendor_id`.
- Creating, renaming, activating or deactivating a Vendor must keep historical Direct Entry facts resolvable and must not manufacture a team membership.
- Project and Vendor mutations are server/RPC authorized. Browser state is never the permission boundary.

### Labor types

- Reuse `direct_entries.labor_type` as the persisted stable catalog key; do not turn it into arbitrary free text and do not rewrite historical entries.
- Seed the canonical keys `TEMPORARY`, `PERMANENT` and `OUTSOURCED`, displayed as `Thời vụ`, `Chính thức` and `Gia công` respectively.
- Admin/Accounting may add, rename, order and activate/deactivate labor-type catalog rows through audited server/RPC mutations. A referenced type is never hard-deleted, and deactivation only removes it from new-entry choices.
- `labor_type` remains independent from `provider_type`, recruiter provider membership and team attribution. Selecting `Gia công` must not imply Vendor or a particular team/provider.
- Direct Entry, change requests, imports/templates and reporting dimensions must resolve the same catalog projection; unknown or inactive values fail closed for new writes while historical values remain readable.

### Accounts, grants and links

- P3.1 manages application accounts that already exist: enable/disable, grant/scope intervals and verified recruiter links.
- Disabling/offboarding revokes effective access without deleting the app user, recruiter identity, facts or audit history.
- Account invitation/creation and recovery credentials remain P3.2 even when the personnel catalog record is created in P3.1.
- No authorization decision may depend on role name, email suffix, client-supplied actor fields or environment fallbacks.

## 4. Reuse and minimum delta

| Need | Reuse | Minimum required delta |
|---|---|---|
| Personnel | `recruiters`, personnel code/position, provider memberships | Audited create/update/activate RPCs |
| Team membership | `teams`, `recruiter_team_memberships` | Audited interval mutations supporting zero-or-one active team |
| Team leader | Verified recruiter links + `team` scope grants | One atomic designation/revocation RPC and policy cardinality |
| Project | `direct_entry_projects`, P2.5 project RPCs and manager history | Admin list/forms and later contracted metadata only |
| Vendor | `vendors`, Vendor recruiter/provider projection | Audited lifecycle RPCs and UI |
| Labor type | Existing `direct_entries.labor_type`, Direct Entry selectors/imports and reporting dimension | Audited catalog table/projection plus active-value validation; seed `OUTSOURCED` / `Gia công` |
| Accounts/grants | App users, capability grants, scope grants, actor repository | Admin-only server mutations and revocation-safe UI |
| Audit/reason/OCC | Restricted reasons, revisions, audit events, RPC idempotency | Catalog entity projections; no new audit framework |
| UI | AppShell/nav registry, Radix primitives, existing grid/forms | One capability-filtered Admin area |
| Validation/tests | Zod, `node:test`, PGlite and existing DB fixtures | Allowed/denied and history regression cases |

No new ORM, RBAC framework, form/state framework, grid library, workflow engine or audit dependency is allowed without a source-backed gap and Owner approval.

## 5. Execution plan

### P3.1-C01 — Catalog administration policy lock

- **Owner:** T1C read-only survey; T0 decisions.
- **Lock:** Accounting catalog-only authority, Admin security authority, leader cardinality, unassigned-person behavior, labor-type key/name/activation rules, field lists and audit/retention rules.
- **Gate:** no token/predicate ambiguity and no path by which Accounting can manage accounts/grants or obtain `pii_export` accidentally.

### P3.1-W01 — Personnel, team and leader backend

- **Owner:** T1B.
- **Build:** audited personnel/team lifecycle RPCs, optional membership, effective move/unassign and atomic leader/team-scope designation.
- **Gate:** forged actor denial, interval non-overlap, history preservation, immediate leader-scope revocation and idempotent replay.

### P3.1-W02 — Project, Vendor and labor-type backend

- **Owner:** T1B, sequentially after W01.
- **Build:** reuse/complete P2.5 project operations; add Vendor lifecycle RPCs; add the audited labor-type catalog and active-value projection; expose bounded list/detail projections.
- **Gate:** no hard delete, no Vendor→team relationship, project/provider/labor-type independence, historical fact resolution and one canonical labor-type source for writes/imports/reporting.

### P3.1-W03 — Existing user, grant and link backend

- **Owner:** T1B.
- **Build:** Admin-only enable/disable, capability/scope interval and verified recruiter-link mutations; `entry_restore` server path; bounded audit projections.
- **Gate:** Accounting denied, stale sessions revoked according to W08A, no client actor authority and no PII leakage.

### P3.1-W04 — Catalog administration UI

- **Owner:** T1A after W01–W02 contracts stabilize.
- **Build:** personnel/team/leader/project/vendor/labor-type list, create/edit, assign/unassign, activate/deactivate and reason/OCC conflict UX; Direct Entry and import/template surfaces consume the active labor-type projection.
- **Gate:** capability-filtered navigation, mobile/a11y, no authority inferred in the browser and no duplicated validation framework.

### P3.1-W05 — User access, audit and restore UI

- **Owner:** T1A after W03.
- **Build:** existing-account status, grants/scopes, recruiter links, audit explorer and `entry_restore`; `pii_export` remains hidden unless Owner explicitly activates it.
- **Gate:** Accounting cannot reach security-admin mutations; denied direct requests fail server-side.

### P3.1-I01/J01 — Integration and Production acceptance

- **Owner:** T1C verification; controlled integration/release by the assigned release lane; T0 acceptance.
- **Verify:** all allowed/denied paths, effective-date history, leader revocation/session freshness, audit immutability, project/vendor/labor-type history, migration rollback and combined Direct Entry/Dashboard regression.
- Only T0 may declare `P3.1_PASS`.
